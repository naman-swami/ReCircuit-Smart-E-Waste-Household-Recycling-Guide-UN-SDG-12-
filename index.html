Science & Metallurgical Reality

Why Recycling 1 Tonne of Phones Beats Mining 100 Tonnes of Rock

Primary gold ore mined from deep underground yields only 1 to 5 grams of gold per tonne of rock blasted and chemically leached with cyanide. In contrast, 1 tonne of retired smartphone circuit boards contains approximately 300 to 350 grams of gold, plus 100 kg of copper and 3 kg of silver.
