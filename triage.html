LIFE-SAFETY CRITICAL • UN3480 EMERGENCY PROTOCOL

The "Red Box" Lithium Battery Thermal Protocol

A swollen, bulging, hissing, or overheating lithium battery is experiencing internal cell shorting and gas buildup. It can undergo thermal runaway (>600°C) within seconds, releasing toxic hydrofluoric acid gas and shooting jet flames. Follow these 4 immediate containment imperatives.

01

DO NOT Charge or Puncture

Immediately unplug from chargers. Never pierce, press, or attempt to "flatten" a swollen battery pouch. Puncturing the separator triggers instantaneous thermal ignition.

02

Tape Terminal Contacts

Wrap non-conductive PVC electrical tape or duct tape completely over exposed battery terminals and connector pins to prevent friction shorts.

03

Dry Sand Container

Submerge the battery in a metal or ceramic bucket filled with dry sand, cat litter, or baking soda. NEVER use water — lithium reacts violently with water forming hydrogen gas.

04

10m Outdoor Perimeter

Move the container outside to a ventilated, non-flammable area (concrete patio, driveway) at least 10 meters away from structures. Transport directly to hazardous waste.
