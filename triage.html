    LIFE-SAFETY CRITICAL • UN3480 EMERGENCY PROTOCOL

    The "Red Box" Lithium Battery Thermal Protocol

    A swollen, bulging, hissing, or hot lithium battery is experiencing internal shorting and electrolyte gas buildup. It can undergo thermal runaway (>600°C) in seconds. Follow these 4 containment imperatives immediately.

    ⚠️ CRITICAL NOTICE: NEVER USE WATER ON SWOLLEN CELLS • DO NOT PUNCTURE • DO NOT ATTEMPT TO CHARGE
    01

    Do NOT Charge or Puncture

    Unplug immediately. Never pierce, press, or attempt to "flatten" a swollen pouch cell. Puncturing the separator triggers instantaneous thermal ignition.

    02

    Tape Terminal Contacts

    Wrap non-conductive PVC electrical tape or duct tape completely over exposed contacts, pins, and wires to eliminate external spark bridges.

    03

    Dry Sand Containment

    Place inside a metal or ceramic bucket filled with dry sand, cat litter, or baking soda. NEVER use water — lithium reacts with water forming flammable hydrogen gas.

    04

    Outdoor Isolation & HHW

    Move the container outdoors away from combustible structures (10m perimeter). Transport directly to a municipal Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) depot.
