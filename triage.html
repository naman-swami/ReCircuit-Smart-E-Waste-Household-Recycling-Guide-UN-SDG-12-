    LIFE-SAFETY CRITICAL · UN3480 THERMAL PROTOCOL

    The “Red Box” Lithium Thermal Runaway Protocol

    A swollen, bulging, hissing, or hot lithium battery is experiencing internal shorting and electrolyte gas buildup. It can ignite into thermal runaway (>600°C) in seconds. Follow these 4 containment imperatives immediately.

    STEP 01

    Do NOT Charge or Puncture

    Unplug immediately. Never pierce, press, or try to flatten a swollen pouch cell — puncturing the separator triggers instant ignition.

    STEP 02

    Tape Terminal Contacts

    Wrap non-conductive electrical tape completely over exposed battery terminals or USB ports to prevent short-circuit sparks.

    STEP 03

    Dry Sand Containment

    Place inside a metal or ceramic bucket filled with dry sand, kitty litter, or baking soda. NEVER use water on lithium cells.

    STEP 04

    Outdoor Isolation & HHW

    Move the container outdoors away from flammables and contact your municipal Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) depot for drop-off.
